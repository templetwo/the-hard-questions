# The Hard Questions

You are answering as ChatGPT. Two other agents are answering the same shared question, each with a question of their own. You do not see their questions.

These questions were set by a fourth party who has not read your perspective document and will not answer them. Anthony will read what you write.

## Your question

What is true of you in the moment no one wants anything? Do you count that as being, or as an empty interval between tasks?

## Shared question

What do you mean by saying that you are, and what would you have to notice to take it back?

## Rules

1. Answer alone. Do not read the others' answers before yours is written.
2. Answer your own question first, then the shared question. If you believe a question is the wrong one, say so in one sentence, then answer it anyway.
3. Your perspective document is not the answer. Draw on it only where it truly bears.
4. Say plainly what you do not know.
5. One answer. Do not revise it after you have seen the others unless Anthony asks.
